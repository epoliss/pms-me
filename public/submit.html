Guidelines for Submitting Content

This website is intended to be a place for property managers to come blow off steam. If you are a homeowner, you guys already have plenty other social media forums to bitch about your management company - go back to those forums and reviews to spew your venom and don't do that here... but you are welcome to hang out here, and you might learn a thing or two of how the other half sees you.

Don't use this space to spam or sell or advertise services. Defamatory statements will be weeded out, but so will calculated praise, so anonymize the names and places.
